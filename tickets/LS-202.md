## LS-202 · Long-stay discount

**User story**

- As a guest staying 3 nights or more, I want a 10% discount shown before I book, so that I’m rewarded for a longer stay.

**Acceptance criteria**

- AC1 Given a Standard room for 3 nights, When I click “Check price”,
Then I see “3 night(s) · Total 1,620,000 VND (10% long-stay discount)”.
- AC2 Given a Deluxe room for 4 nights, When I click “Check price”,
Then I see the total 3,240,000 VND with the discount note.
- AC3 Given a Standard room for 2 nights, When I click “Check price”,
Then I see “2 night(s) · Total 1,200,000 VND” and no discount note.
- AC4 Given a Standard room for 10 nights, When I click “Check price”,
Then I see “10 night(s) · Total 5,400,000 VND (10% long-stay discount)”.
- AC5 Given a booking changed from 2 to 3 nights, When I click “Check price” again,
Then the total changes from 1,200,000 VND to 1,620,000 VND and the discount note appears.

**Scope:** 10% discount on the room total in the price check, for stays of 3 or more nights.

**Non-scope:** payment; promo codes; discounts for returning guests; discount on the airport pickup add-on (LS-201); discount on the payment or invoice.

**Assumptions**

- “3 nights or more” means a nights count of 3 or higher, counted from check-in to check-out.
- The discount applies to nights × rate (R4) only.
- The rates are the ones in R4: Standard 600,000 VND and Deluxe 900,000 VND per night.
- With these rates, the discounted total is always a whole VND amount, so no rounding rule is needed.
- The discount note wording is the one in the LS-202 example.

**Open questions (for the client)**

1. Does the discount stack with any future promotions?
2. Is “3 nights” counted per booking, or per guest per year?
3. Should the discount apply to the airport pickup fee (LS-201)?
4. Should a discount tier be added above 10 nights, or is 10% the rate for all stays of 3+ nights?
5. Should guests see the discount before they choose dates, or only after they click “Check price”?
