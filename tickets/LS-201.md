## LS-201 · Airport pickup add-on

**User story**

- As a guest arriving at Da Nang airport for a Lantern Stay booking, I want to add airport pickup and see its cost before I book, so that I know my total without arranging a taxi myself.

**Acceptance criteria**

- AC1 Given a Standard room for 2 nights and 2 guests, with “Airport pickup” unchecked, when I click “Check price”,
Then I see “2 night(s) · Total 1,200,000 VND” and no pickup line.
- AC2 Given the same booking, When I check “Airport pickup” and click “Check price”,
Then I see “Airport pickup · F VND,” and the total is 1,200,000 + F VND. (F = fee, see OQ1.)
- AC3 Given Deluxe for 3 nights, 4 guests, and pickup checked, When I click “Check price”,
Then the room part is 2,700,000 VND (3 × 900,000) and the total is 2,700,000 + F VND.
- AC4 Given guests set to 0 or 7, When I click “Check price”,
Then I see a validation error and no total.
- AC5 Given check-in of 26 Oct 2026 (yesterday), When I click “Check price”,
Then I see a validation error and no total.

**Scope:** pickup checkbox on the booking form; fee shown in the price check and added to the total.

**Non-scope:** drop-off trips; flight number capture; driver tracking; payment; promo codes; changing a pickup after booking.

**Assumptions**

- The airport is Da Nang International Airport (DAD).
- Pickup is one trip from the airport to the homestay on the check-in date.
- The checkbox is opt-in, unchecked by default.
- The fee is a flat amount per booking, not per guest or per night.
- Amounts are shown in VND only.

**Open questions (for the client)**

1. What is the pickup fee F, and is it per trip or per booking?
2. Does the fee change for larger groups?
3. Is a flight time or flight number needed, and is it required?
4. Does the 10% long-stay discount (LS-202) apply to the pickup fee too?
5. Is a return drop-off offered, and how is it priced?
6. Do local Vietnamese guests see the same option and price?
7. Is tax or a service charge added to the fee?
8. Can pickup be cancelled separately from the room booking?
