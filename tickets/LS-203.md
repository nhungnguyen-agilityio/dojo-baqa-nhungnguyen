## LS-203 · Sign in with Zalo (phase 2)

**User story**

- As a local Vietnamese guest, I want to log in with my Zalo account, so that I can book without creating a new password.

**Acceptance criteria**

- AC1 Given I am logged in with Zalo and open the booking form, When I enter 2 guests for a Standard room for 2 nights and click “Check price”,
Then I see “2 night(s) · Total 1,200,000 VND”, the same as for a guest who is not logged in.
- AC2 Given I am logged in with Zalo, When I enter 0 or 7 guests and click “Check price”,
Then I see a validation error and no total, as for any guest (R1).
- AC3 Given I am logged in with Zalo, When I enter a check-in date of 26 Oct 2026 (yesterday), When I click “Check price”,
Then I see a validation error and no total (R2).

**Scope:** letting a guest sign in to the booking form with a Zalo account; the booking form itself does not change.

**Non-scope:** pricing changes; payment; Zalo messaging or notifications; linking a Zalo account to an existing email account; sign-in for foreign guests.

**Assumptions**

- The brief does not mention login, Zalo, or LS-203. These criteria assume only that the booking rules (R1 to R4) still apply after a guest logs in.
- Zalo login is for local Vietnamese guests, since Zalo is mainly used in Vietnam.
- Login is optional, so guests can still book without signing in.
- The login step does not change the price in any way.

**Open questions (for the client)**

1. Is Zalo login required to book, or optional?
2. What does a guest get after logging in: saved details, booking history, or both?
3. What happens for a guest with no Zalo account, and do they still see the booking form?
4. If a guest logs in with Zalo and later uses a different device, must they log in again?
5. Which data does Lantern Stay need from Zalo (name, phone, email), and does the guest have to agree before sharing it?
6. Is a Zalo login needed for foreign guests too, or only for local guests?
7. Does Lantern Stay already have a Zalo developer app, or must one be set up?
8. How long should a login last before the guest has to sign in again?
